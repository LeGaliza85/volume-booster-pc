Volume Booster - Warning

Loud audio can damage your hearing and your equipment.

This app raises the system volume far beyond what Windows allows, up to 1000 %
(+20 dB). At that level you can damage your hearing, and you can damage
speakers, headphones or amplifiers that were never meant to take it.

You decide how much gain to apply. The author is not liable for hearing damage,
damaged equipment, or any other harm caused by using this software or by playing
audio too loud.
